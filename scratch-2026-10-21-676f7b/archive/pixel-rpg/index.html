A LETTER

for you, from me

My mahal,

I've started this letter more times than I can count. Every version felt too small for what I actually want to say, so I'm just going to say it plainly: I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for what I said, and for what I didn't say when it mattered. I'm sorry for the way I made you feel. Unheard, and alone with something I should have been holding with you. You never deserved that. Not for a second.

I keep thinking about our evenings. The sky going pink, then purple, and how everything felt soft and quiet when you were next to me. That's the version of us I want to protect. That's the version of me I want to be for you.

And I think about Switzerland, and the look in your eyes when you talk about it. I want to be the one standing beside you when you finally see those mountains. Not just there, but everywhere before it.

I'm not writing this to rush you into forgiving me. Take all the time you need. I'll be here, doing the work. Listening more, reacting less, and showing you instead of just telling you.

You are my favourite person. My calm. My home. I'm so sorry, mahal.

Forever yours, your person.

One last thing.

Hold this tulip for me, and don't let go.
